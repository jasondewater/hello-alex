Practical note for an eight-hour Vision Pro run

The program can run eight hours, but Vision Pro is not a sleep mask. Apple recommends regular breaks, and the browser must remain active. For an all-night experiment, the most meaningful parts are the conditioned audio cue and brief dim-red cue windows. The app keeps the long middle sections black and quiet rather than flashing for hours.
