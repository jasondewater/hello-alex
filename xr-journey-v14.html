Photosensitivity warning

This app flashes light across your whole field of view. Flashing lights, rapidly changing colors, high contrast and a large field of view (as in a headset) can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, including people who have never had one.

No rapid-flash setting here has been established as safe. Multicolor mode is not a safety improvement. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.

More information: Epilepsy Foundation: photosensitivity and seizures

Launch

Inside the headset, pinch anywhere or use the Digital Crown to stop immediately. The STOP, PAUSE, RESUME and RESET buttons work on this page; they are not expected to be visible inside the immersive view. RESET stops any run in progress, clears elapsed time, pulse phase and last-run statistics, keeps your settings, and does not start a new run. If the headset view is hidden or blurred by the system, the run pauses and only resumes when you press RESUME here. Closing or leaving this page stops the run.
