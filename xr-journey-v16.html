Photosensitivity warning

This app flashes light across your whole field of view. Flashing lights, rapidly changing colors, high contrast and a large field of view (as in a headset) can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, including people who have never had one.

No rapid-flash setting here has been established as safe. Multicolor mode is not a safety improvement. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.

More information: Epilepsy Foundation: photosensitivity and seizures

Program brightness

85%
Move left for dimmer light throughout the program. In a color journey this limits both the colors and their white phase; in Original red it scales the authored red levels. It does not change the rhythm, tone volume or travel speed. Overall brightness can reduce the light further.

Descent, hold and return

These three numbers divide your chosen time into slowing down, staying at the hold rate, and returning. For example, 40 / 40 / 20 divides 10 minutes into 4 / 4 / 2 minutes. The map below shows the actual rate and time for your settings.

Leave at 100 for neutral white between colors. Lower values tint that entire reference phase toward the current color; 0 shows the same color in both phases. This reduces the color difference, with no fade between phases. The white phase stays within the selected light ceiling.

Diagnostics and direct shortcut

Inside the headset, pinch anywhere or use the Digital Crown to stop immediately. The STOP, PAUSE, RESUME and RESET buttons work on this page; they are not expected to be visible inside the immersive view. RESET stops any run in progress, clears elapsed time, pulse phase and last-run statistics, keeps your settings, and does not start a new run. If the headset view is hidden or blurred by the system, the run pauses and only resumes when you press RESUME here. Closing or leaving this page stops the run. The journey never replaces the working XR clock with the ordinary webpage clock.

Depth views center where you are looking when you enter. RECENTER VIEW uses your next tracked view; while paused, it waits for RESUME and keeps your place in the journey. These are page controls. In Apple Vision Pro, press and hold the Digital Crown for visionOS recentering. That system control is separate from this app; its WebXR behavior depends on Safari/visionOS.
